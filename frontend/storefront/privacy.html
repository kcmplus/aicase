Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

CHRONO CONSULTING CORP operates popomoshop.com under the POPOMO brand. You can use the site to design, order and print custom phone cases. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the website, including when you sign in with Facebook or Google.

Information we use

  • Accounts and sign-in. We use your email address, display name, sign-in records and session information to manage your account. If you use Facebook or Google sign-in, we use the account identifier supplied by that service to recognize your account. Depending on the service, permissions and information available, we may also receive your name and email address. We use those details for sign-in and account setup. We may ask you to verify your email to link accounts.
  • Photos and designs. We use the photos and design choices you provide to show previews and fulfill your requests. Drafts may be saved on this browser. If you request AI generation or place an order, the images needed for that request are sent to our servers for processing.
  • Orders, billing and delivery. We use order details, print artwork, payment references and payment status to fulfill orders and handle support or refunds. For courier delivery, we use the recipient name, phone number and delivery address. The payment provider handles card details.
  • Location and site operation. If you allow location access when looking for nearby print stations, the site uses your coordinates in the browser to calculate approximate straight-line distances. Session cookies and browser storage support sign-in, drafts and order access. Technical information such as IP address and request times helps us operate the service, limit abuse and investigate errors.

Who processes information

Google and Meta process your information when you select their sign-in methods. Our hosting and email providers support the service and deliver verification codes. If you request AI generation, the configured AI provider processes the submitted photo and generation instructions. Stripe or Airwallex processes payments where available. We share the information needed to complete an order with the relevant print or delivery provider.

How long information is kept

We keep account information to operate your account. We retain order and payment records as needed to fulfill orders, handle returns or disputes, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Temporary AI image files are subject to expiration. Copies you save on your device or in your browser can be removed there.

Request deletion of your data

  1. Email 13342827869@163.com with the subject POPOMO data deletion request. Include the email used for your POPOMO account and tell us whether you want to remove your Facebook sign-in connection and related Facebook data or delete your POPOMO account. If you only have a guest order, include its order reference.
  2. We may contact you to verify your identity so we can locate the correct account or order. Keep passwords, verification codes and payment card details private.
  3. After verification, we will process the request and confirm the outcome by email. We will explain if some order or payment records need to be retained for legal obligations, an active order, a dispute or fraud prevention.

You can also remove POPOMO from Facebook’s Apps and Websites settings to revoke its Facebook access. To remove data that POPOMO already holds, follow the request steps above.

Questions and requests

For access, correction, deletion or other privacy questions, contact POPOMO. We post updates to this policy on this page.

隐私政策

更新日期为 2026 年 11 月 18 日

CHRONO CONSULTING CORP 以 POPOMO 品牌运营 popomoshop.com，为顾客提供手机壳设计、下单与打印服务。本政策说明我们如何处理你使用网站时的信息，包括通过 Facebook 或 Google 登录时的信息。

我们使用的信息

  • 账号与登录。我们使用你的邮箱、昵称、登录记录和会话信息管理账号。通过 Facebook 或 Google 登录时，我们用该平台提供的账号标识识别你的账号。根据平台、授权和可用信息，我们也可能获得姓名和邮箱，用于登录和建立账号。关联账号时可能需要验证邮箱。
  • 照片与设计。我们使用你提供的照片和设计内容来显示预览、完成你的请求。设计草稿可能保存在当前浏览器。你主动使用 AI 生成或提交订单时，处理请求所需的图片会上传到服务器。
  • 订单、付款与配送。我们使用订单信息、打印图片、付款编号及状态完成订单、售后和退款处理。快递订单需要收件人、电话和配送地址。银行卡资料由支付服务商处理。
  • 定位与网站运行。如果你在查找附近打印网点时允许定位，网站会在浏览器中使用你的坐标计算大致直线距离。会话 Cookie 及浏览器存储用于登录、草稿和订单访问。IP 地址、请求时间等技术信息用于保障服务运行、防止滥用和排查问题。

服务提供方

选择第三方登录时，相应的 Google 或 Meta 服务会处理相关信息。托管和邮箱服务商用于网站运行及发送验证码。使用 AI 生成时，当前选用的生成服务商会处理你提交的照片和生成指令。付款由可用的 Stripe 或 Airwallex 处理。履行订单所需的信息会提供给相关打印或配送服务商。

保存时间

我们保存运营账号所需的信息，并按履约、售后、争议处理、防止滥用及法律义务所需保留订单和支付记录。临时 AI 图片有有效期。保存在你设备或浏览器上的副本可以在本机删除。

申请删除用户数据

  1. 发送邮件至 13342827869@163.com，主题填写「POPOMO 数据删除申请」。提供你在 POPOMO 使用的邮箱，并说明是要解除 Facebook 绑定并删除相关 Facebook 数据，还是删除整个 POPOMO 账号。如只以访客身份下过单，请附订单编号。
  2. 我们可能联系你确认身份，以便找到正确的账号或订单。请妥善保管密码、验证码和银行卡资料。
  3. 身份确认后，我们会处理申请并通过邮件回复结果。如因法律义务、处理中订单、争议或防欺诈需要保留部分记录，我们会说明。

你也可以在 Facebook「应用和网站」设置中移除 POPOMO，撤销 Facebook 授权。要删除 POPOMO 已持有的数据，请按上述方式提出申请。

联系我们

如需查询、更正、删除个人信息，或有其他隐私问题，请联系 POPOMO。更新后的政策会发布在本页。
